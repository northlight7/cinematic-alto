Safety is
the quiet part.

Route cards left with the hut warden, a check-in schedule, and a hard turnaround time on every summit day. If the weather closes, we turn around. The view is not worth a bad decision.

Every guide carries a group shelter, a satellite messenger and a first aid kit. Guests carry a day pack and a head torch.

  • Maximum group8 guests
  • Guide ratio1 guide per 4 guests
  • TurnaroundFixed, and not negotiable
  • EmergencySatellite messenger on every route
  • FitnessAble to walk 6 hours with a light pack

Do I need mountaineering experience?+

No. The Quiet Valley is a walking route. The Ridge Line and the First Light ask for hill fitness and a head for exposure, not technical skill.

What if the weather turns?+

The route changes or the day is cancelled. The guide decides, and the decision is not up for a vote.

Is this a real company?+

No. ALTO is a fictional brand built to demonstrate web design and development. No trip can be booked.

What should I pack?+

Layers, a shell, water, and something to write with. The full kit list arrives with a real itinerary.

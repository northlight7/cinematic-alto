Every journey below is built around a different relationship with height. The Ridge Line earns its view, the Quiet Valley slows down, and the First Light asks you to get up before the sun does.

01

The Ridge Line

Three days from sheltered meadows to a high ridge, with a night in a mountain hut above the cloud line.

02

The Quiet Valley

Two days beside the river. Clearings, cold water and an evening that turns the peaks blue.

03

The First Light

A dawn ascent to the overlook, then back down for a long breakfast. The shortest way to feel tall.
